The idea in three steps

1. You ask a question in plain English: "How many appointments are scheduled in March?"

2. An AI drafts the database query that would answer it. You see exactly what it wrote.

3. A safety gate checks the query before anything runs. Nine rules block destructive or snooping queries, private columns are removed from answers, and every decision is written to a log. Only queries that pass are executed.

Why this matters

AI-generated SQL is powerful but risky: one wrong or malicious query could delete records or expose private data.

QueryGuard makes the risk visible and controlled: nothing runs unchecked, nothing private leaks, and the numbers on the Metrics page prove it.
